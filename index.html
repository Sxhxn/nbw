Everything that gets you back on the road

From a stone chip caught early to a full windscreen replacement, side mirrors, headlamps and a tired battery — one call, fixes all.

Windscreen repair & replacement

Star break or full crack — we assess it on-site and fit OEM-quality glass, bonded with Sika adhesive for your peace of mind.

Chip repair

Caught in time? A resin repair stops a small chip from spreading, in under 30 minutes.

Side mirrors, cut to size

Cracked or missing mirror glass replaced and cut to fit your exact make and model.

Headlamp restoration

Cloudy, yellowed lenses polished back to clear — better light, better looks.

Willard battery supply

Free load-testing, and fitment of the correct SABS-approved Willard battery for your vehicle.

Fleet, corporate & insurance claims
